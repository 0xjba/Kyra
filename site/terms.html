3. Pawtrol

Pawtrol is an optional subscription that turns Kyra into an autonomous background agent. It:

  • scans your storage on a schedule you choose, and when free space runs low;
  • automatically clears safe caches that apps regenerate on their own, according to rules you can configure;
  • flags riskier items, such as Docker data, AI models, Xcode archives and the Trash, for you to review instead of removing them on its own;
  • scores what is worth cleaning through our server. That needs an internet connection; when the server can't be reached, Kyra scores locally.

All cleaning happens on your Mac. What Pawtrol sends to our server is described in the Privacy Policy.
